== Quality Requirements

// Quality tree and concrete scenarios. A scenario is testable: a
// stimulus, the system's reaction, and a measurable response. The
// top goals are summarised in chapter 1.

v0 is a feasibility study; the scenarios are its success criteria.
They are checked by hand on reference courses: at least two real
courses, one with several past exams and one with at most one.

=== Quality Tree

* *Exam readiness*
** Topic coverage (Q1)
** Material preparation (Q2)
** Guided learning (Q3)
** Cheatsheet (Q4)
** Exam level (Q5)
* *Cost efficiency*
** Ingestion cost (Q6)
** Tutoring cost (Q7)
* *Honesty*
** Gradability tiers (Q8)

=== Quality Scenarios

[cols="1,3,4", options="header"]
|===
| # | Stimulus | Response

| Q1
| A reference course is ingested.
| Every task of every past exam falls under a topic. No topic that a
  reviewer finds in the past exams or in `notes.md` is missing, and
  no topic is split or duplicated.

| Q2
| A reviewer compares the topic artifacts with the PDFs.
| Priority matches the past exams. The sources of every topic name
  each of its tasks with the right pages, including the figures and
  solutions they need, and no page without a task of the topic.

| Q3
| The student works through a topic in several sessions.
| The tutor teaches from what the student cannot do yet to exam-style
  tasks. Every session starts where the last one ended, as recorded
  in `progress.md`; `progress.md` names what was practised, with
  assessed and self-reported signals apart.

| Q4
| The student works through several topics without asking for the
  cheatsheet.
| `cheatsheet.md` contains the key terms, formulas, and solution
  strategies that came up, grouped by topic, without duplicates.

| Q5
| The student asks for harder and harder tasks.
| Posed tasks stay within the difficulty and working time of the
  course's past exam tasks. Once the exam level is reached, the tutor
  says so instead of going beyond it.

| Q6
| A reference course is ingested.
| The ingestion costs at most 5 €.

| Q7
| The student prepares a reference course to exam readiness.
| All chats and finalizations of the course together cost no more
  than its ingestion.

| Q8
| A reviewer checks the gradability tier of the tasks in the topics'
  sources.
| No Tier C task is posed as Tier A or B. A reviewer finds no Tier A
  task classified as Tier B.
|===
