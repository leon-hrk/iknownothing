== Risks and Technical Debt

// Known risks and accumulated debt, ordered by priority, each with
// what would mitigate it. This chapter is for problems we already
// carry, not decisions we have not made yet.

=== Risks

[cols="1,2,2", options="header"]
|===
| Risk | Impact | Mitigation

| Topics depend on the order of the chunks
| Every exam and exercise pass sees only its chunk and the topics
  so far. A topic first seen in a later chunk can end up split from,
  or duplicated with, an existing one.
| Every pass gets all current topics and may change them. A final
  pass over the topics alone that merges duplicates removes the risk.

| Missing lecture content
| Open points are written from exams and exercises alone. Lecture
  content no open point asks for is missing for the tutor, which then
  teaches in its own words and notation.
| `topic.md` lists the open points the lecture does not answer, and
  the tutor marks what it explains on them.

| Misclassified gradability tier
| Tiers are assigned by the model without review. A Tier C task
  classified as Tier B is posed and cannot be answered; a Tier A task
  classified as Tier B loses a graded signal.
| Checked by hand on the reference courses (Q8).

| Wrong topics
| Topics are built without review. A missing, split, or misnamed
  topic propagates into progress, and fixing it means re-ingesting
  the course. Material no topic takes up is visible nowhere.
| `notes.md` feeds the exam passes. Topic review (chapter 1, out of
  scope) removes the risk.

| Shared rate limit and budget
| All users run on one API key. Parallel ingestions exhaust its rate
  limit and slow down every chat; one user's heavy use spends the
  budget of all.
| The worker concurrency limits parallel jobs; a spend limit on the
  key caps the cost. Per-user keys (chapter 1, out of scope) remove
  the risk.

| Finalization lost on tab close
| On tab close the transcript is sent as a `keepalive` request, which
  browsers cap at 64 KiB and may drop. A long chat or a killed
  browser loses its progress update.
| Accepted for v0. The cheatsheet is unaffected; it is updated during
  the chat.
|===
