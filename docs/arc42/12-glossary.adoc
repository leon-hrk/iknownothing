== Glossary

// Domain and technical terms, so the same word means the same thing
// in the UI, the code, and this document.

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Term | Definition

| Assessed signal
| A done Tier A task in `progress.json`: the tutor graded it, and its
  note says how it went. Counts as evidence.

| Block
| A paragraph of a source conversion, or one of its page markers,
  numbered from 1 per file. Topic sources name their tasks as ranges
  of blocks.

| Cheatsheet
| `cheatsheet.md`: key terms, formulas, and solution strategies the
  student has encountered. A living artifact, updated by the tutor
  during chats. Read-only for the student.

| Course
| The top-level unit, owned by one user: sources, notes, derived files,
  and student state for one exam.

| Course-level chat
| A session scoped to the whole course, for orientation and planning.
  Loads the notes, the progress of every topic, the topic priorities
  and tasks, and the cheatsheet, never a topic artifact.

| Chat tool
| An action the tutor takes during a chat through a tool call:
  `update_cheatsheet`, `pose_task`, `complete_step`.

| Derived file
| A file generated from the sources: their conversions,
  `topics.json`, the topic artifacts, and the intermediate results in
  `ingestion/`. Written once by ingestion;
  read-only for the student.

| Document type
| Exercise sheet or past exam. Given by the subdirectory the operator
  puts the file in, stored as the subdirectory of `sources/`, and the
  basis of source priority.

| Gradability tier
| The class of a task by how the tutor can handle it. *Tier A*: posed
  and graded. *Tier B*: posed, self-assessed. *Tier C*: out of reach,
  not posed, named to the student.

| Input file
| A file the pipeline takes as given: the course PDFs and
  `notes.md`, provided by the operator.

| Ingestion
| The pipeline that turns a course's PDFs into Markdown, topics with
  their sources, topic directories, and the topic list.

| Living artifact
| A file derived from the student rather than from the material:
  `cheatsheet.md` and `progress.json`. Both are updated by the tutor
  during chats.

| Notes file
| `notes.md`: free-form remarks about the course, which the AI must
  respect. Required, provided by the operator when the course is
  added, and read-only afterwards.

| Operator
| The person who runs an instance, provides its Anthropic API key,
  creates its users, and adds the courses of its students.

| Progress
| `topics/<topic>/progress.json`: whether the introduction to one
  topic was given, and every Tier A and B task of it with whether it
  is done and a note on how it went. A living artifact.

| Self-reported signal
| A done Tier B task in `progress.json`: its note is the student's
  own rating, marked as such. Not evidence, and never presented as
  assessed.

| Session
| One chat: course-level or topic-level. Stored until the student
  ends it; several can be open in tabs at once.

| Source
| A course PDF in `sources/`.

| Source conversion
| The Markdown of a source, `sources/<type>/<file>.md`, with the
  figures the OCR cut out next to it.

| Source priority
| The ranking of document types. For what a topic is: past exams >
  exercises. For solution style and notation: exercise solutions >
  past exam solutions > exam question.

| Step
| A part of a topic-level session: the introduction, or one task.
  Recorded in `progress.json` with `complete_step` when it is done;
  the chat then continues without its messages.

| Student
| The user of the tutor. Owns courses.

| Student state
| The progress files, `cheatsheet.md`, and the token usage files.
  Derived from the student, not from the material.

| Topic
| A unit of exam content, defined by what the exam asks. Carries a
  priority.

| Topic artifact
| `topics/<topic>/topic.md`: description, exam relevance, priority,
  and the topic's sources, with the Tier C tasks named as out of
  reach.

| Topic directory
| `topics/<topic>/`: the topic artifact and its progress.

| Topic list
| `topics.json`: the topics in priority order, each with its priority,
  topic directory, and sources. Orders the topic tree, gives the
  course-level chat the priorities and tasks, and a topic-level chat
  the block ranges of its tasks.

| Topic source
| One task of a topic in the exams or exercises: its ID, its label,
  its gradability tier, its fit - `loose` for an exercise task that
  only possibly belongs to the topic - and its block ranges, each in
  one conversion -
  the task, the figures, tables, and data it refers to, and its
  solution. They are loaded into the topic's topic-level chat.

| Topic-level chat
| A session scoped to one topic, for tutoring and quizzing.

| Unit
| The files of one past exam or one exercise sheet with its
  solutions, which carry the same number in their names. Topic
  extraction and task assignment read one unit per request.

| v0
| The first version. Its scope is chapter 1; what it does not include
  is listed under Out of Scope there.
|===
