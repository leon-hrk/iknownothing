== Glossary

// Domain and technical terms, so the same word means the same thing
// in the UI, the code, and this document.

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Term | Definition

| Assessed signal
| A mastery signal in `progress.md` from a task the tutor graded
  (Tier A). Counts as evidence.

| Cheatsheet
| `cheatsheet.md`: key terms, formulas, and solution strategies the
  student has encountered. A living artifact, updated by the tutor
  during chats. Read-only for the student.

| Course
| The top-level unit, owned by one user: sources, notes, derived files,
  and student state for one exam.

| Course-level chat
| A session scoped to the whole course, for orientation and planning.
  Loads the notes, the progress of every topic, the topic priorities
  and tasks, and the cheatsheet, never a topic artifact.

| Chat tool
| An action the tutor takes during a chat through a tool call:
  `update_cheatsheet`, `pose_task`.

| Derived file
| A file generated from the sources: `topics.json`, the topic
  artifacts, and the intermediate results in `ingestion/`. Written once by ingestion;
  read-only for the student.

| Document type
| Exercise sheet or past exam. Chosen on upload,
  stored as the subdirectory of `sources/`, and the basis of source
  priority.

| Finalization
| Updating a topic's `progress.md` from a topic-level chat. Happens
  when the chat ends: the student opens another chat or closes the
  tab.

| Gradability tier
| The class of a task by how the tutor can handle it. *Tier A*: posed
  and graded. *Tier B*: posed, self-assessed. *Tier C*: out of reach,
  not posed, named to the student.

| Input file
| A file the pipeline takes as given: the uploaded PDFs and
  `notes.md`.

| Ingestion
| The pipeline that turns a course's PDFs into topics with their
  sources, topic directories, and the topic list.

| Living artifact
| A file derived from the student rather than from the material:
  `cheatsheet.md` and `progress.md`. The cheatsheet is updated during
  chats, progress on finalization.

| Notes file
| `notes.md`: the student's free-form remarks about the course, which
  the AI must respect. Required, uploaded when the course is created,
  and read-only afterwards.

| Operator
| The person who runs an instance, provides its Anthropic API key,
  and manages its accounts.

| Progress
| `topics/<topic>/progress.md`: the student's mastery of one topic,
  with assessed and self-reported signals kept apart. A living
  artifact.

| Self-reported signal
| A mastery signal in `progress.md` from the student's own rating of a
  Tier B task. Not evidence, and never presented as assessed.

| Session
| One chat: course-level or topic-level. Exists only in the browser
  while it is open; one is open at a time.

| Source
| An uploaded PDF in `sources/`.

| Source priority
| The ranking of document types. For what a topic is: past exams >
  exercises. For solution style and notation: exercise solutions >
  past exam solutions > exam question.

| Student
| The user of the tutor. Owns courses.

| Student state
| The progress files and `cheatsheet.md`. Derived from the student,
  not from the material.

| Topic
| A unit of exam content, defined by what the exam asks. Carries a
  priority.

| Topic artifact
| `topics/<topic>/topic.md`: description, exam relevance, priority,
  and the topic's sources, with the Tier C tasks named as out of
  reach.

| Topic directory
| `topics/<topic>/`: the topic artifact and its progress.

| Topic list
| `topics.json`: the topics in priority order, each with its priority,
  topic directory, and sources. Orders the topic tree, gives the
  course-level chat the priorities and tasks, and a topic-level chat its source
  pages.

| Topic source
| One task of a topic in the exams or exercises: its label, its
  gradability tier, and its page ranges, each in one file - the task,
  the figures and tables it refers to, and an exercise's solution.
  The *source pages* of a topic are loaded into its topic-level
  chat.

| Topic-level chat
| A session scoped to one topic, for tutoring and quizzing.

| v0
| The first version. Its scope is chapter 1; what it does not include
  is listed under Out of Scope there.
|===
